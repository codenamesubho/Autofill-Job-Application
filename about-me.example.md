# About Me

Copy this to about-me.md and fill in real details, then:
  autofill-answer snapshots/<timestamp>.json --context about-me.md

Anything written here is the ONLY source the answering agent will draw from.
It will not guess facts that aren't stated here.

## Basics
Full name, location, email, phone, links (LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolio).

## Experience
Current role, past roles, what you actually did — specific enough to answer
"describe a project you're proud of" honestly.

## Preferences
Relocation, remote/hybrid/onsite, notice period, what you're looking for next.

Do NOT put salary expectations, visa/work-authorization status, or anything
you wouldn't want answered automatically here — those categories are always
withheld and escalated to you regardless of what this file says.
